Privacy policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

Scam Ray is a video channel and a small publishing tool (the "Scam Ray app") that its owner uses to post Scam Ray's own videos. This policy explains what the app does with data.

Who uses the app

Only the channel owner signs in to the Scam Ray app. Viewers of our videos never sign in to it, and it collects nothing from them.

Google data the app uses

  • YouTube (upload scope): to upload Scam Ray's own videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the Scam Ray channel.
  • Google Drive: to save copies of Scam Ray's own videos into the owner's Drive folders so they can be reviewed before posting.

The app does not read, store or share any other YouTube or Drive data, and it does not sell or share data with anyone. Sign-in credentials are kept encrypted as private secrets in the tool's hosting service and are used only to perform the actions above.

Scam Ray's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google's security settings. After revoking, the app can no longer upload or save anything.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or analytics.

Contact

Questions about this policy: scamray@googlegroups.com
